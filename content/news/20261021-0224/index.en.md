From October 16 to 19, Dr. Zhang Haoran and Dr. Niu Mengjiao from the research group attended the Chinese Society of Engineering Thermophysics Conference on Heat and Mass Transfer held in Xi’an. They presented their respective research works titled “Regulation of Thermal Resistance at Moving Interfaces by Velocity and Synchronous Force Measurement” and “Study on Contact Thermal Resistance Based on Lock-in Infrared Technology,” and engaged in academic exchanges with participating experts and scholars.
