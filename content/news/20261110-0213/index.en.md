Professor Wang attended the 7th China New Materials Industry Development Conference. As a co-chair of the conference, he organized the session on thermal management materials and gave an invited talk titled 'Research on Thermal Management Materials and the Thermal Transport Properties of Their Contact Interfaces.' After the conference, Professor Liu Yingjun from Zhejiang University and his team visited the research group's lab and had in-depth exchanges.
