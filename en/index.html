About the Group

Our group works on three research directions: development and application of multiphysics measurement systems, design and optimization of thermal equipment, and intelligent analysis with lightweight deployment. We aim to connect the full chain of precision measurement, optimal design and intelligent analysis. Students interested in interdisciplinary research across mechatronics, energy and artificial intelligence are welcome to join us for research or graduate study, and we welcome exchanges and collaboration with colleagues.
