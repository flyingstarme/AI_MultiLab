Research

Our group works on three research directions: development and application of multiphysics measurement systems, design and optimization of thermal equipment, and intelligent analysis with lightweight deployment. We aim to connect the full chain of precision measurement, optimal design and intelligent analysis.

Development and Application of Multiphysics Measurement Systems

  • Measuring Anisotropic Thermophysical Properties of Micrometer-Thick Films
  • Measuring Thermoelectric Properties and Contact Processes of Micro/Nanostructures
  • Development of a Scanning Thermal Microscopy System Based on T-Shaped Thermal Probes
  • Measuring and Modulating Ultrafast Energy Transfer in Semiconductor Nanofilms

Design and Optimization of Thermal Equipment

  • Intelligent Optimization Design Methods and Platform for Thermal Equipment
  • Multi-Objective Collaborative Optimization Design of Liquid-Cooling Equipment
  • Vapor-Liquid Two-Phase Transport Mechanisms and Performance Regulation of Flat Micro Heat Pipes
  • Design of Downhole Electric Heating Equipment for Oil and Gas Resources
  • Engineering Application: Multiphysics-Coupled Thermal Management of Underwater Energy Storage Battery Compartments

Intelligent Analysis and Lightweight Deployment

  • Technical Framework and Research Platform for Intelligent Analysis and Lightweight Deployment
  • Magnetic Flux Leakage Signal Reconstruction and Generative Sample Augmentation
  • Intelligent Prediction and Operating Condition Diagnosis for Oil and Gas Production Data
  • Lightweight Models and Real-Time Edge Deployment
  • Interactive Data Visualization and Intelligent Operation and Maintenance Platform
